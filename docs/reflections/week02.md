## Reflection
I personally think that AI cannot fully define what it means to be human on its own. Because much of it is run on an algorithm, it may only be based on that rather than branching out to make decisions. AI cannot think; this is something we know. If it cannot think for itself and is rather using other information, it cannot on its own tell us what it means to be human.
